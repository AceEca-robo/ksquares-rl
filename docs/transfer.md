## Parity probe

Positions where nothing can be taken, safe lines remain and the move decides the result (long chain rule). 10x7 / 7x7: new sets from games of ladder agents with 1-2 safe lines, exact values by the judge (strong engine to the end; 10x7 15.0 s, 7x7 4.0 s per position; only positions it solved: with more safe lines, or the app's 1 s, it solves almost none on 10x7); 5x5: the 1000 positions of docs/parity_probe_positions.json (1-6 safe lines), and its subset with 1-2 safe lines. Policy: the argmax is a correct move. V after the move: the sign of the value head after every legal move is right (balanced: mean of the shares over winning and over losing moves; 50 = blind).

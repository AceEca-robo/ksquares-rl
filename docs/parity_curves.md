(nothing to capture, safe lines left, the move decides who opens the first
long chain; every position has winning and losing moves, no ties). Every
accepted generation of az5 and az5b; plot `docs/parity_curves.png` (x = the
accepted generations in order).

- policy: the policy argmax is a winning move (random legal move 16.8%);
- V child: sign of the value head after every legal move (mover's view) is
  right, balanced accuracy over winning / losing moves (chance 50%); in
  brackets right after winning / after losing moves;
- V greedy: the move with the best child V wins (one-ply search with the value head);
- MCTS: the MCTS 200 move wins (no noise, temperature 0).

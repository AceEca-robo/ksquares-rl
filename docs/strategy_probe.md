- **dd** (double-dealing): a box can be taken, but every capturing line loses (or ties) where the best move wins: the mover must decline the last two boxes of a chain (four of a loop) — the all-but-two / all-but-four trick.
- **parity** (long chain rule): nothing can be taken, safe lines remain, and the choice decides who has to open the first long chain; the fix can be a safe line or a sacrifice.
